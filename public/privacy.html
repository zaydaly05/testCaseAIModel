Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Test Case Generator (at test-case-ai-model-plum.vercel.app) turns user stories into test cases. This page explains what data the app accesses when you use it, especially when you sign in with your Atlassian account, and what happens to that data.

What we access when you sign in with Atlassian..

Signing in is optional. If you do, Atlassian asks you to approve these permissions:

  • Read Jira issues (read:jira-work): used only to fetch the stories you ask for, by issue key, by a JQL query, or with "Pull all stories".
  • Read your Jira profile (read:jira-user): used only to show your display name ("Signed in as …").
  • Offline access: lets the app renew your sign-in without asking you to log in again every hour.

The app also receives the list of Jira sites your account can access, so you can choose which one to use. It cannot create, change or delete anything in Jira.

How your data is used

Stories you fetch from Jira, or type or paste into the app, are sent to our server only to generate test cases. The model that generates them runs on our own server. Your stories are not sent to any third-party AI service.

What we store

  • Stories and test cases are not stored. They are processed while your request runs and then discarded. Results exist only in your browser tab until you close or reload it, or in files you choose to download.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your own browser as encrypted cookies that the page's scripts cannot read. They are not stored on our servers. They expire after 30 days, and signing out deletes them immediately.
  • We do not create user accounts, and we keep no database of users or Jira data.

Cookies

The only cookies are the ones needed for Jira sign-in described above. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The app is hosted on Vercel, which processes requests to run the app and may keep standard access logs, such as IP address, time and requested URL. Jira data comes directly from Atlassian under Atlassian's privacy policy.

Removing access

Click Sign out in the app's Jira tab to delete the sign-in cookies. To revoke the app's access to your Atlassian account completely, remove it at id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/apps.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
